## How they were made

The characters: from concept images we made ourselves, in Tripo (image to 3D, its Mixamo-compatible rig, and its
preset clips `walk`, `idle`, `sit` and `play_mobile_game`), on Dima's paid plan. The keep: Blender scripts run on his
PC.

## The list

None of them is here yet. They come in with v4.0.0, the Blender models in part 4a and the characters in 4b, and each is
listed here then with its file, its author, how it was made and, for a Tripo character, its ProduceID. Until then they
live on the branch `forge-keep`, in `town/models/`, under its own `LICENSE.md`.
