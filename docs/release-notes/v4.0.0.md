The Forge Keep in 3D: the hall's page is now a walled town on a floating island, where the five agents walk to where their work is, and the page around it is a HUD you work in. Nothing changes in how you run Claude Code, in the team, or in what the plugin may do.

## The Forge Keep

- **A town of nine areas.** War hall, Library, Design studio, Workshop, The Forge, Tavern, Training yard, Courtyard and Garden, joined by paths. Turn it, pan it and zoom in with the mouse; zoomed in, a house's roof lifts off and the walls between you and the room fold down. **Cutaway** opens every house at once.
- **The team in 3D.** The Lead/PM is a goblin, the Designer an elf, the Frontend dev and the Backend dev orcs, the Reviewer a reaper with a green flame. Each walks to where its state says: a dev fetches its task from the War hall and works at its desk in the Workshop, and the Reviewer checks a dev's work beside that dev's desk and reads in the Library.
- **What the keep shows.** Resting agents wander the keep's idle places (never a work room) and stop to talk when two meet. The furnace glows brighter the more agents work. A screen lights only while its owner works at it. An agent that needs you shows a raised hand over its name tag, one that failed a "!".
- **Where 3D can't draw** (no WebGL 2, a model that fails to load, a lost graphics context), the page shows the pixel-art hall of v3, which keeps working as it did.

## The HUD

- **Everything over the keep.** The top bar has the run and its tasks; the rooms on the left fly the camera to a room or the whole keep; the agents along the bottom open each one's card (its model, its look, its state, its task, its tools), with **Follow** to keep it in view as it walks and **Show in Activity**; the drawer on the right has the activity feed and the task board. Name tags and speech bubbles ride over the figures.
- **Needs you.** A banner under the top bar names each agent waiting for your approval in Claude Code, with **Show me**, which flies to it and opens its card.
- **What you look at stays in view.** Show me, Follow and the room views put their target in the middle of the part of the keep the panels leave free, and the view slides there as a panel opens or closes. Name tags never cover each other and stay inside the window; a tag moved off its figure gets a thin line back to it.
- **From the first moment.** The HUD is there while the keep loads ("Raising the keep…"), so the feed, the cards and the banner work before the first frame.
- **On a phone** (the demo on GitHub Pages): the title on one line, the rooms and the activity as buttons in the top bar, and the card as a sheet at the bottom in the agents' place.

## Accessibility

- **Keyboard and screen readers.** Every control is a real button in a fixed order (top bar, status or banner, rooms, keep, agents, card, drawer); Esc closes the rooms menu, the card, then the drawer, the focus going back each time. The agent list says in words everything a name tag shows; the tags themselves are hidden from screen readers, and what a bubble must not miss is read out.
- **Checked with axe-core** over the HUD's states (loading, at work, a card, needs you, the drawer closed, a phone with a card): no serious or critical issue. Text keeps a contrast of 4.5:1, and focus is always visible.
- **Reduced motion** is respected: no wandering, no flicker, sparks or bobbing, and the camera cuts instead of flying.

## Security

- **Nothing new reaches the network.** three.js (0.187.0) and the keep's models are bundled with the hall and served by it; the page's Content-Security-Policy is unchanged (no `unsafe-eval`, no `blob:`), so a model carries no packed image and every texture is a file the hall serves. The hall serves the models only as listed in its manifest, by exact name.
- **Agent text** in bubbles, the card and the feed is still plain text, as in v3.

## Updating from v3.10.0

In a terminal (Git Bash):

```bash
claude plugin marketplace remove world-of-agents
claude plugin marketplace add kremendev/world-of-agents#v4.0.0
claude plugin install world-of-agents@world-of-agents --scope local   # in each project folder
```

then `/reload-plugins` in a session that is already open. Nothing to set up again: your projects' teams, notes, library and settings stay as they are. A v3.10.0 hall still running is stopped and replaced by the new one at your next message. The plugin is about 18 MB now, most of it the keep's models.

Claude Code 2.1.312 or later and Node.js 20.19 or later, as in v3.10.0, and a browser with WebGL 2 for the keep. Setup and the full security notes are in the [README](https://github.com/kremendev/world-of-agents/blob/v4.0.0/README.md).
