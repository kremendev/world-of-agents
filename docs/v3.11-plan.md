**In one line:** the header, the status line, the task bar, the agent panel, the "For you" strip, the activity feed and
the Review board move to the `--ui-*` tokens and Inter that the Team page uses since v3.6; Pixelify Sans stays only
inside the hall.

## What moves (from src/index.css, 16 Nov)

- **Pixelify outside the hall today:** the page title (`h1`) and the header's badge. Both go to Inter. Inside the hall it
  stays exactly where it is: room plaques, name plates, the hall's own badges.
- **The parts:** `.app-header` and the badge, `.status-line` (and the needs-you banner it turns into), `.task-bar`
  (Demo only), `.agent-panel` and `.status-chip`, `.for-you`, `.feed` (head, rows, flags, the "Show all" text, the
  actions), `.review-board` and its cards, the Copy buttons. About 900 of the stylesheet's 1,886 lines.
- **The tokens:** the 21 on `:root` from v3.6 (`--ui-bg`, `--ui-surface`, `--ui-raised`, `--ui-text`, `--ui-muted`,
  `--ui-line`, `--ui-line-strong`, `--ui-accent`, `--ui-on-accent`, `--ui-link`, `--ui-flag`, `--ui-flag-bg`,
  `--ui-danger`, `--ui-danger-fill`, `--ui-on-danger`, `--ui-success`, `--ui-selected`, `--ui-font`, `--ui-mono`,
  `--ui-radius`, `--ui-radius-panel`). The plan adds none unless the mockup needs one, and then names it.

## What stays

- The hall: canvas, art, `HallLabels` (its text keeps its rem minimums), the dashboard, the furnace label.
- Every behaviour and every text: the feed's flags (To you, Needs you, Failed) and how they're set, the cut at 200
  characters, Show all, Copy and what it removes, the review format, the Review board's focus and inert rules, the
  aria-live region, read marks. This version changes how things look, never what they do or say.
- The layout rule that the whole hall fits the first screen, and that the activity column goes under the hall when beside
  it would cost the hall over 15%.

## The mockup (from Claude, before anything is built)

What it must show, the look being its call within the tokens: the whole page in Live with a run going (header with the
plugin's version and "This PC" line, the status line, the hall, the feed with a To you row, a Needs you row and an opened
long message, the For you strip), the needs-you banner, the Review board open over the feed with two reviews, the agent
panel, the Demo variant with the task bar, and the narrow layout (activity under the hall). Dima approves it before 3.11b.

## Accessibility

Contrast at least 4.5:1 for text and 3:1 for lines and focus rings, checked on every token pair the page uses (a test
computes them from the stylesheet); focus visible on every control; sizes in rem, nothing under 13 px (the layout test's
rule stays, with Pixelify allowed only under `.hall`); reduced motion unchanged. Narrator over the feed, the For you
strip and the Review board, as in v3.6's check.
